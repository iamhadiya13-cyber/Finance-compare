## Introduction

If you earn around ₹30,000 per month, you are probably wondering whether a personal loan is realistic and exactly how much you can get approved for.

Many people in this salary bracket ask the same questions: Is ₹30,000 enough? Will banks actually give me a decent amount without making my budget tight?

The answer depends on a few key things, mainly your credit score, existing EMIs, and job stability. The good news is that with careful planning you can get a useful loan amount while keeping your monthly payments comfortable.

Most lenders follow similar income and EMI rules, but approval criteria can vary slightly. Use an [EMI calculator](/calculators/personal-loan-emi-calculator) to see the exact impact on your budget before you apply.

## Quick Answer

### Loan Eligibility on ₹30,000 Salary

A person earning ₹30,000 per month can typically get a personal loan of ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh, depending on credit score, existing EMIs, and job stability.

## How Much EMI Can You Afford on ₹30,000 Salary?

Lenders usually follow the 30–40% rule: your total EMIs, including the new personal loan, should not cross 30–40% of your net monthly salary.

On ₹30,000 take-home pay, this means a safe EMI range of ₹9,000 to ₹12,000 per month.

Staying within this limit leaves enough for rent, food, transport, and unexpected expenses. Crossing 40% can make life difficult if your income does not grow quickly.

## Loan Amount Based on EMI

Your affordable EMI directly decides the loan amount you can take. Here are practical examples, assuming average interest rates and 36–60 month tenure:

- ₹10,000 monthly EMI → roughly ₹3–4 lakh loan
- ₹12,000 monthly EMI → roughly ₹4–5 lakh loan

## Realistic Loan Scenarios

### Case 1: No existing EMI + good credit score (750+)

You are in the strongest position. Lenders may approve up to ₹4.5–5 lakh because your full EMI capacity is available.

### Case 2: Existing EMI already running

If you already pay for a bike loan, credit card EMI, or another debt, your available room drops. You may get approved for ₹2.5–3.5 lakh only.

### Case 3: Low credit score (below 700)

Approval chances reduce and the amount offered is usually smaller. In some cases you may face rejection until the score improves.

## Where You Can Get a Loan

At ₹30,000 salary, both traditional banks and NBFCs consider applications.

- **Banks** are usually stricter but may offer slightly lower interest rates if your profile is clean
- **NBFCs and digital lenders** are often more flexible with salary thresholds and faster processing, though rates can be higher

Choose based on the interest rate, repayment comfort, and processing speed that works for you.

## Factors That Affect Approval

Apart from salary, lenders look at:

- **Credit score (CIBIL):** Higher score means stronger approval chances
- **Job stability:** At least one year in the current job helps
- **Employer type:** A reputed employer can improve your profile
- **City:** Metro cities sometimes get slightly higher limits due to cost of living

Your credit score also affects your eligibility. Read this guide on [how credit score affects your personal loan amount](/blog/how-credit-score-affects-personal-loan-amount-india).

## Should You Take the Maximum Loan?

Even if you are eligible for ₹5 lakh, taking a slightly lower amount can reduce financial stress and make repayment more comfortable.

Always choose a loan amount that fits your monthly budget, not just the maximum a lender is willing to approve.

## FAQ

### Can I get a ₹5 lakh loan on ₹30,000 salary?

Yes, it is possible if you have a good credit score, no existing EMIs, and a stable job. Most people in this salary range get approved for ₹3–4.5 lakh.

### What EMI is safe for ₹30,000 salary?

₹9,000 to ₹12,000 per month is considered safe. This keeps your total debt within 30–40% of income.

### Is ₹30,000 salary enough for a personal loan?

Yes. Many lenders accept applications at this level, though the approved amount will be moderate compared to higher salaries.

### Does city matter for ₹30,000 salary eligibility?

Slightly. Metro cities sometimes get better limits because of higher living costs, but the difference is usually small.

### Can I increase the loan amount later?

It is difficult. It is better to apply for the right amount in the first application based on your current eligibility.
