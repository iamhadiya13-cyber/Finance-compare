## Introduction

Your credit score, often referred to as CIBIL score in India, is one of the biggest factors lenders check when deciding your personal loan eligibility.

It tells them how reliably you have repaid past loans and bills. A good score can mean a higher loan amount and lower interest rate. A low score can reduce the amount you get or lead to rejection, even if your salary is strong.

## Quick Answer

### How Credit Score Affects Personal Loan Eligibility

Your credit score directly affects whether you get approved, how much you can borrow, and the interest rate you receive.

## Credit Score Ranges

Here is how lenders generally view CIBIL scores for personal loans:

- **750 and above** → Best approval chances and highest loan amounts
- **700–750** → Good approval with decent amounts
- **650–700** → Limited options and smaller loan amounts
- **Below 650** → Very difficult to get approved; many lenders reject outright

## How Score Affects Loan Amount

Lenders use your credit score to decide the income multiplier they apply.

- A 750+ score often unlocks the full multiplier, sometimes around 20–24 times monthly salary
- A score below 700 usually reduces the multiplier, so you may get a lower loan amount even on the same salary

For example, on a ₹30,000 salary:

- 750+ score → ₹4–5 lakh possible
- 650 score → ₹2–3 lakh or less

The same pattern repeats across every salary bracket.

## Real Examples

- ₹25,000 salary + 750+ score → up to ₹3–4 lakh
- ₹25,000 salary + 650 score → ₹1.5–2 lakh or rejection
- ₹50,000 salary + 750+ score → up to ₹10–12 lakh
- ₹50,000 salary + 650 score → ₹5–7 lakh maximum

Even a small improvement in credit score, for example from 680 to 730, can significantly increase your loan amount and reduce your interest rate.

## How Score Affects Interest Rate

Higher credit score usually means lower interest rate.

- 750+ score → you may get rates in the 10.5–13% range
- Below 700 → rates can jump to 16–24% or more

Over the full loan tenure, a lower rate can save you a meaningful amount in total interest.

## Why Lenders Care

From the lender’s perspective, a low credit score signals higher risk of delayed or missed payments.

To protect themselves, lenders may reduce the loan amount, charge higher interest, or reject the application entirely.

## How to Improve Credit Score

You can improve your score over a few months with consistent habits:

- Pay every EMI, credit card bill, and installment on or before the due date
- Keep credit card utilisation below 30% of your limit
- Avoid applying for too many loans or credit cards in a short period
- Check your CIBIL report periodically and correct any errors quickly

## FAQ

### Does a 700 credit score get me a personal loan?

Yes, but the amount and interest rate are usually lower than what borrowers with 750+ scores receive.

### Can I get a loan with a low credit score?

It is possible with some NBFCs, but the loan will usually be smaller and more expensive.

### How long does it take to improve my credit score?

Consistent on-time payments often show visible improvement in around 3–6 months.

### Will one missed EMI destroy my score?

One late payment can hurt, but consistent good behaviour afterwards can help repair the damage over time.

### Should I check my score before applying?

Yes. Always check your CIBIL report first so you know what to expect and can correct any issues.
