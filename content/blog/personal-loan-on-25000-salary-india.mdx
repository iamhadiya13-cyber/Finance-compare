## Introduction

If you earn ₹25,000 per month, you may be wondering whether lenders will even consider your application and how much you can realistically get.

Many first-time borrowers in this salary range have the same doubt: Is this salary enough for a personal loan?

The answer is yes, it is possible, but the amount will usually be modest and the EMI must be planned carefully so it does not become a burden.

Most lenders follow similar income and EMI rules, but approval criteria can vary slightly.

## Quick Answer

A person earning ₹25,000 per month can typically get a personal loan of ₹1.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh, depending on credit score and existing EMIs.

## EMI Capacity on ₹25,000 Salary

Lenders usually prefer that your total EMIs stay within 30–40% of your net salary. On ₹25,000 take-home pay, this means a safe monthly EMI range of ₹7,000 to ₹10,000.

Staying in this range matters because your income already covers basic expenses like rent, food, and transport. A higher EMI can quickly create stress if something unexpected happens.

Here are practical examples assuming average interest rates and 36–60 month tenure:

- ₹9,000 monthly EMI → ₹3–4 lakh loan

## Reality Check

At ₹25,000 salary, approval is stricter than at higher income levels. Many traditional banks set higher minimum salary cut-offs, so you may get fewer options from them.

NBFCs and digital lenders are more common at this salary and are often willing to consider applications, though interest rates can be slightly higher.

## Where You Can Get a Loan

- **Banks:** Possible only if your profile is very clean and your salary is credited to them, but limits are usually lower
- **NBFCs and digital lenders:** More flexible and often faster for this salary bracket

Set realistic expectations. The loan will usually be smaller and the rate may not be the lowest available.

## Factors That Matter More at Low Salary

At this income level, lenders look even more closely at:

- **Credit score:** A good score becomes very important because there is less income buffer
- **No existing EMIs:** Every current EMI reduces what you can borrow
- **Job stability:** At least one year in the current job helps show reliability

Your credit score plays a major role. Read [how credit score affects your personal loan amount](/blog/how-credit-score-affects-personal-loan-amount-india).

## Tips to Improve Your Chances

- Pay all bills and existing EMIs on time to maintain a good credit score
- Avoid taking fresh debt or overspending on credit cards before applying
- Apply with the bank that receives your salary
- Choose a longer tenure if it keeps EMI manageable

## FAQ

### Can I get a personal loan with ₹25,000 salary?

Yes, many lenders accept applications at this level, though the approved amount is usually modest.

### What is a safe EMI on ₹25,000 salary?

₹7,000 to ₹10,000 per month is generally manageable for most borrowers.

### Will I get rejected because of low salary?

Not necessarily. A strong credit score and stable job can still help you get approved.

### Is it better to apply with an NBFC at this salary?

Often yes. NBFCs are usually more flexible than many banks for lower salary brackets.

### Can I increase the loan amount later?

It is difficult. It is better to apply for the right amount the first time.
